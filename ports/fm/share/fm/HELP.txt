fm — analog FM from Term49 (OpenMAX AL)

  fm                 availability + help
  fm avail           check without tuning
  fm 95.1 [sec]      tune and play (default: until Ctrl-C)
  fm seek [up|down]  seek and print the stop
  fm now [95.1]      frequency, strength, stereo, RDS
  fm scan            seek up around the band
  fm status          open radio, dump current
  fm off             this CLI is process-local (Ctrl-C the player)

Freq: 95.1 or 95100 (kHz). Headset is usually the antenna.

Install: qpkg install fm
