CAPTAIN'S LOG — STARDATE 47634.44
USS Enterprise NCC-1701-D


Captain's Log, Stardate 47634.44.

We have entered the Devron system in response to what appears to be a
spatial anomaly. Long-range sensors have detected unusual readings that
warrant closer investigation.

The crew remains on high alert. Commander Data has been running
continuous scans since we entered the system, attempting to determine
the nature of the anomaly.


SUPPLEMENTAL — STARDATE 47634.51

The anomaly appears to be some form of temporal disruption. Initial
readings suggest it may be related to the anti-time phenomena we
encountered near the Neutral Zone.

I have ordered all departments to submit status reports and have
convened a senior staff meeting for 0800 hours.


SUPPLEMENTAL — STARDATE 47634.78

Counselor Troi reports that several crew members have experienced
unusual dreams and a sense of displacement. Medical is investigating.

Commander La Forge has proposed modifying the main deflector to emit
an inverse tachyon pulse. If successful, this may allow us to scan the
interior of the anomaly more thoroughly.


PERSONAL LOG — STARDATE 47634.85

There are times when I question the nature of our mission out here.
We seek out new life, new civilizations — but at what cost? How many
of our crew have we lost to the unknown?

And yet, I would not trade this life for any other. The stars call to
us, and we must answer.

End log.
