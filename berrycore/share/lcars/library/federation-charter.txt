CHARTER OF THE UNITED FEDERATION OF PLANETS

PREAMBLE

We the lifeforms of the United Federation of Planets determined to save
succeeding generations from the scourge of war, and to reaffirm faith in
the fundamental rights of sentient beings, in the dignity and worth of
all lifeforms, in the equal rights of members of planetary systems large
and small, and to establish conditions under which justice and respect
for the obligations arising from treaties and other sources of
interstellar law can be maintained, and to promote social progress and
better standards of life in larger freedom, and for these ends, to
practice tolerance and live together in peace with one another as good
neighbors, and to unite our strength to maintain interstellar peace and
security, and to ensure, by the acceptance of principles and the
institution of methods, that combative force shall not be used, save in
the common interest, and to employ interstellar machinery for the
promotion of the economic and social advancement of all combatant
peoples, have resolved to combine our efforts to accomplish these aims.

ARTICLE ONE

The Organization is based on the principle of the sovereign equality of
all its Members.

ARTICLE TWO

All Members shall settle their interstellar disputes by peaceful means
in such a manner that interstellar peace and security, and justice, are
not endangered.

ARTICLE THREE

All Members shall refrain in their interstellar relations from the
threat or use of force against the territorial integrity or political
independence of any planetary system, or in any manner inconsistent with
the Purposes of the United Federation.

ARTICLE FOUR

Membership in the United Federation of Planets is open to all
peace-loving planetary systems which accept the obligations contained in
the present Charter and, in the judgment of the Organization, are able
and willing to carry out these obligations.

ARTICLE FIVE

The admission of any such planetary system to membership in the United
Federation will be effected by a decision of the Federation Council upon
the recommendation of the Admiralty.
