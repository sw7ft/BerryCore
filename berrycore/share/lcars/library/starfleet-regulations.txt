STARFLEET GENERAL ORDERS AND REGULATIONS


GENERAL ORDER 1 — THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

No starship may interfere with the normal development of any alien life
or society. This directive takes precedence over any and all other
considerations, and carries with it the highest moral obligation.


GENERAL ORDER 4

No Federation vessel shall enter the Romulan Neutral Zone without
explicit authorization from Starfleet Command.


GENERAL ORDER 6

If all combatant personnel have perished, the computer shall assume
command of the vessel and attempt to return to a Federation starbase.


GENERAL ORDER 7

No vessel under any condition, emergency or otherwise, is to visit
Talos IV. Violation of this order is the only offense remaining on the
books that is punishable by death.


GENERAL ORDER 12

On the approach of any combatant vessel, all Federation starships shall
determine the identity of said combatant and take appropriate defensive
action.


GENERAL ORDER 15

No commanding officer shall leave their vessel unless they are assured
of the safety of their crew.


GENERAL ORDER 24

Upon issuance of this order, a starship shall destroy all life on an
entire planet. This order has never been executed.


REGULATION 46-A

If transmissions are being monitored during a mission, the use of
uncoded messages is prohibited.


REGULATION 157, SECTION 3, PARAGRAPH 18

Starfleet officers shall not interfere with the internal affairs of
other civilizations.


PROTOCOL 28, SUBSECTION D

In the absence of commissioned officers, command falls to the highest
ranking crew member on duty.
