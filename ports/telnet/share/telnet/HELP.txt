Telnet 0.17 — classic TELNET client (netkit)

Install:  qpkg install telnet
Binary:    /accounts/1000/shared/misc/bin/telnet
Docs:      share/doc/telnet/MANUAL.md

Quick use:
  telnet 192.168.1.179
  telnet 192.168.1.179 8080

Escape to command mode:  Ctrl+]  then  quit

Note: client only, not encrypted — prefer SSH when available.
