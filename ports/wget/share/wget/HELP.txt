GNU Wget 1.25.0 for BlackBerry 10
==================================

HTTPS file downloader — included in BerryCore core packages.

QUICK START
-----------
  wget https://example.com/
  wget -O file.zip https://example.com/file.zip
  wget -O- https://example.com/ | head

HTTPS
-----
  Wrapper sets SSL_CERT_FILE to BerryCore ssl/cert.pem
  or share/wget/certs/cacert.pem.

UPGRADE
-------
  qpkg update   (installs wget-1.25.0.zip with BerryCore upgrade)

VERSION
-------
  GNU Wget 1.25.0 (replaces 1.20.3)
