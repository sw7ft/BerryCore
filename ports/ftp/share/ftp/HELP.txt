FTP 1.0.0 — netkit ftp client + bftpd server (BerryCore bundled)

Client:  ftp hostname
Server:  cp $NATIVE_TOOLS/etc/bftpd.conf.example $NATIVE_TOOLS/etc/bftpd.conf
         bftpd -c $NATIVE_TOOLS/etc/bftpd.conf -D   (default port 8021)
         Note: bftpd may need root/privileged user to bind on BB10 — client works fine.
Share:   $NATIVE_TOOLS/ftp-root/
Logs:    $NATIVE_TOOLS/var/log/bftpd.log
Docs:    $NATIVE_TOOLS/share/doc/ftp/MANUAL.md

Security: plain FTP — home LAN only. Prefer SSH/SFTP for sensitive files.
