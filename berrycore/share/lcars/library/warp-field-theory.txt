WARP DRIVE FUNDAMENTALS
Technical Manual — Starfleet Engineering


CHAPTER 1: SUBSPACE MECHANICS

The warp drive functions by generating warp fields to form a subspace
bubble that envelops the starship, distorting the local spacetime
continuum and moving the starship at velocities that exceed the speed
of light.

The cochrane is the unit of measure for subspace distortion. One
cochrane equals the field strength necessary to achieve a velocity of
one times the speed of light (c). The unit is named for Zefram Cochrane,
who conducted the first successful warp flight on Earth in 2063.


CHAPTER 2: WARP VELOCITIES

Traditional Scale (TOS Era):
  Warp 1  =    1c
  Warp 2  =    8c
  Warp 3  =   27c
  Warp 4  =   64c
  Warp 5  =  125c
  Warp 6  =  216c

Revised Scale (TNG Era):
  Warp 1  =      1c
  Warp 5  =    214c
  Warp 6  =    392c
  Warp 7  =    656c
  Warp 8  =  1,024c
  Warp 9  =  1,516c
  Warp 9.9 = 3,053c

Warp 10 is considered infinite velocity — occupying all points in the
universe simultaneously. It remains theoretical.


CHAPTER 3: MATTER/ANTIMATTER REACTION

The warp core operates by combining deuterium (matter) with antideuterium
(antimatter) in a controlled reaction mediated by dilithium crystals.
The resulting plasma is channeled to the warp nacelles, where it powers
the warp field coils.

Reaction Formula:
  D + antiD → Energy (via dilithium regulation)

Dilithium is the only known element capable of being rendered porous to
antimatter when subjected to high-frequency electromagnetic fields.


CHAPTER 4: STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

At warp velocities, the structural integrity field (SIF) maintains the
physical cohesion of the vessel. Without this field, the vessel would
be torn apart by the stresses of the warp bubble's formation.

The inertial dampening field (IDF) compensates for acceleration forces
that would otherwise flatten the crew against bulkheads during rapid
maneuvers.


CHAPTER 5: SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Emergency core ejection procedures are to be followed when antimatter
containment failure is imminent. The warp core can be ejected from the
engineering hull to prevent total vessel destruction.

Never exceed warp 9.9 for extended periods. Subspace damage may result.
